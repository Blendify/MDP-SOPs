Soft Goods Equipment
********************

The Soft Goods Equipment section documents the standardized procedures for preparing, handling,
deploying, maintaining, and storing all **soft goods and fabric-based equipment** used by Maryland
Productions (MP) and Event Revolution (RV). Soft goods are highly visible scenic elements and must be
treated with care to maintain a clean, professional appearance and safe operation.

These SOPs ensure soft goods remain clean, undamaged, correctly sized, and show-ready for every
event.


Purpose
=======

- Standardize preparation and handling of all soft goods.
- Maintain professional appearance and cleanliness.
- Prevent damage from improper handling or storage.
- Ensure safe installation and use of fabric-based elements.
- Extend the service life of MP/RV soft goods.
- Support efficient warehouse organization and deployment.


Who This Section Is For
======================

- Staging and scenic technicians  
- Lighting technicians installing drape or scenic fabric  
- Warehouse staff prepping and resetting soft goods  
- Rigging staff interfacing with pipe-and-drape or flown fabric  
- Project Managers overseeing scenic scope  
- Freelancers handling MP/RV soft goods  

Anyone touching, hanging, folding, or storing soft goods must follow the procedures in this section.


Scope of Soft Goods Equipment
=============================

This section covers SOPs for:

- Pipe and drape systems
- Stage skirts and facades
- Scenic drape and theatrical curtains
- Backdrops and scenic drops
- Table linens and coverings
- Velour, IFR, and specialty fabrics
- Soft goods hardware (bases, uprights, crossbars)
- Storage bags, racks, and carts for soft goods


Soft Goods Equipment Lifecycle
==============================

All soft goods equipment follows the same standardized lifecycle:

1. **Prep**
   - Inspect fabric for damage or excessive wear
   - Verify correct sizes and quantities
   - Confirm flame-retardant (IFR) status where required
   - Pack clean and folded properly

2. **Deployment**
   - Transport in protective bags or cases
   - Install using approved hardware
   - Avoid contact with dirty or abrasive surfaces

3. **Operation**
   - Monitor for sagging, movement, or damage
   - Protect from spills, weather, and heat sources
   - Maintain clean appearance throughout the event

4. **Strike & Return**
   - Remove carefully to avoid tearing or dragging
   - Inspect during strike
   - Scan back into inventory

5. **Reset & Storage**
   - Clean or launder as required
   - Repair or retire damaged items
   - Fold and store according to standards
   - Return to designated storage locations

Soft goods must not be shelved without proper reset.


Soft Goods Standards
====================

All soft goods must meet the following standards:

- Clean and free of visible stains or odors
- No tears, burns, or excessive fraying
- Correct size and color for the intended use
- IFR compliant where required by venue or code
- Properly labeled with size, type, and ownership (MP / RV)

Soft goods not meeting these standards must be removed from service.


Handling & Cleanliness
======================

- Always handle soft goods with clean hands.
- Never drag fabric on the floor.
- Keep away from sharp edges and hot fixtures.
- Avoid contact with liquids, food, or adhesives.
- Do not place soft goods directly on dirty surfaces.

Appearance matters.


Integration with Other Departments
==================================

Soft goods equipment interfaces closely with:

- **Lighting** – Uplighting, backlighting, and scenic integration
- **Rigging** – Pipe-and-drape systems and flown fabric
- **Staging** – Stage skirts and scenic facades
- **Power** – Clearance from heat and cabling
- **Logistics** – Packing order and protection during transport

Coordination ensures clean and safe installations.


Common Soft Goods Issues
=======================

Soft goods problems most often arise from:

- Improper folding or storage
- Moisture exposure
- Contact with sharp or hot equipment
- Incorrect sizing or quantity
- Skipping cleaning after use

The linked SOPs address these risks directly.


Structure of Soft Goods Equipment SOPs
======================================

Each Soft Goods SOP includes:

- Overview and intended use
- Safety and IFR considerations
- Prep and deployment procedures
- On-site handling expectations
- Cleaning, repair, and retirement standards
- Storage and labeling requirements

This ensures consistency and ease of reference.
